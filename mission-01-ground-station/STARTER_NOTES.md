# Starter notes

The repository gives you a starting point rather than a completed solution.

Use official YAMCS and Open MCT documentation when you need details about installation or configuration.

For this challenge, `telemetry.csv` is the source data. You do not need to build a physical RF link.

A good first milestone is simply: the data is interpreted correctly, useful parameters are visible, trends can be plotted, and another person can understand the screen without asking you what everything means.
